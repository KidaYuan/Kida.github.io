Construction sites are tough environments for wireless networks. Moving machinery, metal structures, dust, and changing layouts create constant signal challenges. At the Torre Norte project in Barcelona, Spain, we delivered a reliable **integrated indoor and outdoor WiFi solution** that supports both office work and field operations.

### Project Challenges
- Frequent layout changes and heavy metal interference
- Need for stable coverage in offices, dorms, work zones, and perimeter areas
- Reliable video surveillance transmission
- Harsh outdoor conditions (dust, heat, weather)
- Easy scalability as the project progresses

Traditional setups often suffered from dead zones, poor roaming, and unstable connections.

### Solution Architecture
We deployed a layered **Comfast** wireless system with a central gateway, PoE switches, high-power outdoor APs, ceiling APs for indoor spaces, and dedicated wireless bridges for remote cameras.

**Key Equipment Used**:
- **CF-AC101** Gigabit Core Gateway for unified management
- **CF-SG181P** PoE Switches for power and data distribution
- **CF-EW87 & CF-WA971** Outdoor WiFi 7 APs (3600Mbps) for wide-area coverage
- **CF-E373BE** Ceiling WiFi 7 APs for seamless indoor roaming
- **CF-E312A V2** Wireless Bridges for stable 5.8GHz video backhaul

### Key Outcomes
- Full indoor-outdoor signal continuity
- Smooth roaming for staff and inspectors
- Stable high-definition video feeds
- Weather-resistant hardware built for construction sites
- Flexible design that adapts to project phases

This practical deployment shows how modern WiFi equipment can solve real-world connectivity problems in demanding environments.
