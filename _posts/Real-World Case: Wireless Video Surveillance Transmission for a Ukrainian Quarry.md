# Real-World Case: Wireless Video Surveillance Transmission in a Ukrainian Quarry

Quarries are challenging sites for network setups. Wide open spaces, uneven terrain, heavy dust, strong winds, and constant truck traffic make traditional cabling expensive and unreliable.

This project at Ukraine’s Kryvyi Kamen Quarry shows how a solid **[5.8 GHz wireless bridge solution](https://wifioem.com/comfast-wireless-bridge-manufacturer)** can deliver stable, real-time video monitoring across the entire operation area.

## Project Background
The quarry covers stone extraction, crushing, stockpiling, and loading zones with significant elevation differences. Previous cameras suffered from unstable signals, difficult cabling due to traffic, dust damage, and coverage blind spots — especially around the weighbridge, crushing area, and night loading points.

**Customer Requirements:**
- Stable, high-quality video transmission to the central control room
- Minimal wired infrastructure and trenching
- Reliable performance in harsh outdoor conditions
- Easy scalability for future cameras

## Solution Overview
We deployed a hybrid wireless system using **[COMFAST](https://wifioem.com/)** wireless bridges for long-distance backhaul and POE switches for local device aggregation. This avoided heavy cabling while ensuring robust connectivity.

**Core Equipment:**
- **CF-E312A V2** & **CF-E319A V2** 5.8 GHz Wireless Bridges — for point-to-point and relay links
- **CF-GF105** 100M Core Gateway — central management
- **CF-SF180P** 8-port POE Switches — local power and connectivity

The topology used a central monitoring room with zoned relay points to overcome obstacles like slopes, stockpiles, and steel structures. A detailed site survey, optimized line-of-sight, elevated mounting, and reinforced installations ensured long-term stability.

## Challenges & Solutions
- **Terrain & obstructions** → Relay stations and height adjustments
- **Dust & weather** → Protected mounting + proper cable management
- **Wind & vibration** → Strong brackets and precise antenna alignment
- **Power & maintenance** → Standardized POE, clear labeling, separated power/data lines

Thorough testing covered day/night conditions, multi-channel stability, and peak operation periods.

## Results
The system now delivers reliable real-time video from key areas including the weighbridge, crushing zone, pit edges, and entrance. Operations are smoother, blind spots are reduced, and management is more efficient. The modular design makes future expansions simple.

This project demonstrates why 5.8 GHz wireless bridging is often the smarter choice for industrial sites — offering flexibility, lower maintenance, and adaptability to changing environments.
